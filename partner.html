Become a Frost Creator

We're looking for streamers and video creators who make Minecraft content and want to team up with Frost. If you post TikToks, YouTube videos, or streams featuring Frost Client, you can get paid for it — not just a shoutout or free merch.

How it works

Everyone starts as a Media creator — sign in, pick a discount code, and you're live in under a minute, no review needed. Grow your numbers and you can request a rank-up to Partner, then Partner+, each with a better commission rate and a shorter wait before you can withdraw.

What you get

Your own discount code

A personal code your audience can use at checkout — every order placed with it is tracked back to you automatically.

Free assets for your content

Ready-to-use banners for your videos and streams, so promoting Frost fits naturally into what you're already making.

Commission on every order

Earn up to 30% commission on every order placed through your code — see exactly how the rate works below.

The process, step by step

1

Sign in and pick a code

Sign in with Discord below, tell us where you'll be promoting, and choose your discount code — that's it, no waiting on a review.

2

Share it

Drop your code in videos, streams, and descriptions. Every order placed with it is credited to you automatically.

3

Earn commission

Watch your orders and estimated earnings roll in on your dashboard in real time.

4

Withdraw

Request a payout to PayPal from your dashboard in a couple of clicks.

5

Rank up

Land 5+ orders and a banner appears on your dashboard — request a promotion to Partner (and later Partner+) for a better rate and a shorter withdrawal wait.

Media vs Partner vs Partner+

Media is fixed and instant. Partner and Partner+ are earned via a rank-up request once you're active — the rate is worked out with staff at that point.

Media
10%

5% customer discount · 10% commission · 2-month withdrawal wait. Sign up instantly below.

Partner
~20%

Negotiated commission · 1-month withdrawal wait. Reached by requesting a rank-up from your Media dashboard.

Partner+
~30%

Negotiated commission · 2-week withdrawal wait · free Frost Client Lite. Reached by ranking up further from Partner.

Getting started

Media has almost no bar to clear — Partner and Partner+ are things you grow into.

Media requires
  • ✓A link to where you'll be promoting Frost
  • !Must not promote cheats — this one's non-negotiable.
Partner rank-up
  • ✓5+ orders placed under your Media code
  • ✓1,000+ followers or subscribers
  • ✓Active account — posts or uploads regularly
Partner+ rank-up
  • ✓Strong order volume as a Partner
  • ✓5,000+ followers or subscribers
  • ✓10k+ avg views per video, or 40+ avg viewers per stream (TikTok)
  • ✓Consistent, regular uploads and an engaged audience

Creator dashboard

Live stats

Total orders, this month's orders, and estimated earnings, plus charts for orders over time and per month.

Instant asset downloads

Withdraw in a few clicks

Full withdrawal history

Every request you've made, whether it's pending, paid, or denied, with a reference ID for each one.
