Become a Frost Partner

We're looking for streamers and video creators who make Minecraft content and want to team up with Frost. If you post TikToks, YouTube videos, or streams featuring Frost Client, you can get paid for it — not just a shoutout or free merch.

📦 — downloads on Modrinth →

How it works

There are two tiers, Partner and Partner+, with different requirements and different payouts. There's no fixed public rate — every deal is worked out individually based on your platform, audience size, and content style. Apply on our Discord and we'll figure out the right reward together.

What you get

🏷️

Your own discount code

A personal code your audience can use at checkout — every order placed with it is tracked back to you automatically.

🖼️

Free assets for your content

Ready-to-use banners for your videos and streams, so promoting Frost fits naturally into what you're already making.

💸

Commission on every order

Earn up to 30% commission on every order placed through your code — see exactly how the rate works below.

The process, step by step

1

Apply

Sign in with Discord below and submit your TikTok or YouTube channel — takes under a minute.

2

Get reviewed

We look at your platform, audience size, and content style, then reach out on Discord with a decision and your tier.

3

Receive your code and assets

Once approved, you get your personal discount code plus the banner assets to start featuring Frost in your content.

4

Share it

Drop your code in videos, streams, and descriptions. Every order placed with it is credited to you automatically.

5

Earn commission

Watch your orders and estimated earnings roll in on your dashboard in real time.

6

Withdraw

Request a payout to PayPal from your dashboard in a couple of clicks.

Partner vs Partner+

There's no fixed public rate for applying — your tier is worked out individually based on your reach and content.

Partner
20%

Commission on every order placed through your discount code.

Partner+
30%

For creators with more reach — a bigger share of every order, plus free Frost Client Lite for the duration of your partnership.

Entry requirements

What we look for before approving an application. Meet the Partner bar and we'll tell you if you also qualify for Partner+.

Partner requires
  • ✓1,000+ followers or subscribers (TikTok or YouTube)
  • ✓Active account — posts or uploads regularly
  • !Must not promote cheats — this one's non-negotiable.
Partner+ requires
  • ✓5,000+ followers / subscribers
  • ✓10,000+ views per video on average
  • ✓Active, engaged audience — not just numbers
  • ✓Consistent, regular uploads
  • ✓Content focused purely on Minecraft — not a mixed channel covering multiple games together

Partner dashboard

Once you're approved, everything lives at partner.frostclient.eu — sign in with the Discord account that holds your Partner role.

✓

Live stats — total orders, this month's orders, and estimated earnings, plus charts for orders over time and per month.

✓

Payment history — every order placed under your code and what it earned you.

✓

Withdraw in a few clicks — enter an amount and your PayPal email on the withdraw page, and submit. No forms, no waiting on Discord for someone to process it manually.

✓

Full withdrawal history — every request you've made, whether it's pending, paid, or denied, with a reference ID for each one.
