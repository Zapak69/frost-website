Become a Frost Partner

We're looking for streamers and video creators who make Minecraft content and want to team up with Frost. If you post TikToks, YouTube videos, or streams featuring Frost Client, you can get paid for it — not just a shoutout or free merch.

How it works

There are two tiers, Partner and Partner+, with different requirements and different payouts. There's no fixed public rate — every deal is worked out individually based on your platform, audience size, and content style. Apply on our Discord and we'll figure out the right reward together.

What you get

Partner

  • ✓Your own discount code
  • ✓20% commission on every order through your code
  • ✓Free assets for your stream

Partner+

  • ✓Your own discount code
  • ✓30% commission on every order through your code
  • ✓Free assets for your stream
  • ★Free Frost Client Lite for the duration of your partnership

Partner — entry requirements

TikTok

  • ✓Minimum 1,000 followers
  • ✓Active account (posts regularly)
  • !Must not promote cheats — this one's non-negotiable.

YouTube

  • ✓Minimum 1,000 subscribers
  • ✓Active channel (uploads regularly)
  • !Must not promote cheats — this one's non-negotiable.
Partner+

For creators with real reach, better rewards.

  • ✓5,000+ followers / subscribers
  • ✓10,000+ views per video on average
  • ✓Active, engaged audience — not just numbers
  • ✓Consistent, regular uploads
  • ✓Content focused purely on Minecraft — not a mixed channel covering multiple games together

Ready to apply?

Sign in with Discord and fill out a quick form — takes under a minute. We'll take a look and work out the reward together from there.

⚠You must be a member of our Discord server to apply — if you're not, we have no way to reach you if your application is accepted.

Creating content on a different platform (Twitch, Kick, etc.)? Open a ticket on Discord instead — TikTok and YouTube go through the form above.
