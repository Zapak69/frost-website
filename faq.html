Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: July 5, 2026

General

What is Frost?

Frost is a free Minecraft client built around speed, customization, and simplicity. It ships with 60+ built-in mods, a friend list, custom capes, a mod browser, and both Vulkan and OpenGL rendering — no manual file installs or mod manager required.

Is Frost really free?

Yes. Frost is, and always will be, free to download and use — no access code, no hidden costs.

Is Frost Client trusted?

Yes. Our previous client, FoxClient, went through a public legit-check on our Discord — the legit-check channel has 600+ reactions from people vouching it was genuinely safe to use, and that same trust carries over to Frost.

Will using Frost get me banned on servers?

Frost is a client-side utility mod, similar to other Minecraft clients. Some servers restrict certain mods or clients in their rules, so always check a server's policy before joining.

How do I move my settings to a new Frost version?

If installing a new version created a separate instance instead of updating your old one in place, see our guide on transferring old settings — it walks through copying your config and options over so you don't lose your setup.

Vulkan vs. OpenGL

What's the difference between Vulkan and OpenGL?

Both are graphics renderers Frost can run on. Vulkan is the newer, lower-overhead option and gives the biggest FPS boost on most modern systems. OpenGL is the older, more broadly compatible option — it runs on more hardware and drivers, just with less of a performance gain.

Which one should I download?

Start with Vulkan for the best performance. If it fails to launch or crashes on startup, that's usually fixable — see our Vulkan setup guide for the Java arguments that solve it on most systems. Prefer not to bother with that at all? Grab the OpenGL build instead, it works with no extra setup.

How do I download the OpenGL version?

On Modrinth, just head to the modpack's Versions tab, then pick your Minecraft version and select the OpenGL build from the list — Vulkan and OpenGL are published as separate versions for each supported Minecraft release.

FrostClient Lite

What do I do after purchasing Lite?

After checkout, you're redirected to our thank-you page — just sign in with Discord there. Once you're signed in, your subscription is verified automatically and you'll get access to download FrostClient Lite right away.

I closed the thank-you page — how do I get my access?

If this happened, go to your Whop dashboard and click Discord. Sign in with Discord there, then click Claim Access — you should get the Lite Discord role right away. If you don't, contact us in a ticket on Discord.

Once you have the role, head to frostclient.eu/lite/download and continue from there.

What's the difference between Frost and Lite?

Lite is a special build focused purely on performance — it pushes optimization further than the regular client, giving on average around 100–400 more FPS than classic Frost, depending on your hardware. On top of that, Lite subscribers get a badge before their nametag in-game, a special Discord role, priority tickets, and staff pings on Discord.

How do I cancel my Lite subscription?

Head to frostclient.eu/lite/cancel — the instructions to cancel are right there.

Installation

Frost is distributed as a .mrpack modpack file through Modrinth. Installing it takes two steps once you have your download.

1

Install the Modrinth App

Frost installs through the Modrinth App — download and install it first if you don't already have it.

2

Download Frost

Click the download button on the homepage — pick your renderer and version and download the .mrpack file from modrinth where you will be redirected.

3

Open the downloaded .mrpack file

That's it — just double-click the downloaded file. The Modrinth App picks it up automatically and installs Frost Client for you, no manual setup required.

4

Launch from the Modrinth App

Once the install finishes, you'll find Frost listed as an instance in the Modrinth App — select it and press Play.

Don't own Minecraft? The official Modrinth App requires a premium (Microsoft-authenticated) Minecraft account. If you want to play on a cracked/offline account, use AstralRinth instead — a fork of the Modrinth App with offline account support. Install it the same way as the Modrinth App above, then follow the same steps: open the downloaded .mrpack file and Frost installs itself inside AstralRinth too.
