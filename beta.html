Official Statement

A New Era for FrostClient

From the entire FrostClient team · Last updated: August 27, 2026

With the launch of our own launcher, FrostClient is entering a new era. Our goal is simple: to take FrostClient to a whole new level — one platform that connects the client, the launcher, capes, Lite and the whole Frost ecosystem into a single, seamless experience.

We are in beta

What many people don't realize: the current versions of the launcher and the client are BETA versions. That means you may — and probably will — run into bugs. When you find one, please report it on our Discord server. Every single report helps us polish the final release and ship it as soon as possible.

The best thing you can do right now is simple: be patient, and report as many bugs as you can. And please skip the unnecessary questions — "when will the launcher be finished", "when is the next update", "why is my game lagging" — answering those over and over only eats the time we could be spending on development.

About updates

Updates take time. We deliberately don't want to push tiny patch updates every other day — instead, we bundle fixes into larger updates that resolve many issues at once. It may look quieter from the outside, but it means every update you get is a meaningful step forward.

Performance & game feel

We know — and we understand — that some older versions may have felt faster and smoother. We're actively working on that too. Tuning the performance and the overall feel of FrostClient so it's the best it can possibly be is one of our top priorities for the final release.

To our Lite subscribers

A huge thank you to every Lite subscriber who is staying with us and believes that everything will fall into place soon. Lite is currently the biggest form of support you can give us — we refuse to put ads into the launcher or onto the website, and Lite is what makes that possible.

Why things take time

Here's the honest truth: the entire development of FrostClient is handled by a single developer — everything you see, and everything behind it, stands on one pair of shoulders. The rest of the staff team takes care of user support and tickets, and they deserve a huge thank you for keeping it all running. On any given day, that one developer is maintaining and developing:

  • The main website
  • The Partner Dashboard
  • The backend
  • Launcher servers
  • Discord bot servers
  • The Discord bot itself
  • The connections across the whole FrostClient ecosystem
  • Promo videos
  • Partner management

Any help counts. If you'd like to help us improve FrostClient and speed up development, we'd love to have you — apply to join our team below.

— Zipp, on behalf of the entire FrostClient team
