Join the FrostClient Team

FrostClient is entering a new era, and we're looking for people who want to help build it — from support to design to content. Pick the position that fits you, sign in with Discord, and tell us about yourself.

Who we're looking for

Video Editors & Social Media

Take care of the videos we share across our social networks — editing, posting, keeping our channels alive.

Graphic Designers

Create designs for the client — branding, UI assets, artwork that shapes how FrostClient looks.

Visualizer

Create visualizations of how the launcher or FrostClient could look — concepts and mockups that guide development.

Staff Team

Pure support — handling tickets, answering questions, and taking care of our users every day.

Cape Designer

Create brand-new capes and designs for the Frost cape collection.

Cape Animator

Bring capes to life — create the animated versions of our cape designs.
