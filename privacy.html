Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

1. Overview

Frost is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the Frost Minecraft Client or visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any of your data, with one exception: while the client is running, it periodically reports a lightweight "still online" signal used to show live player-count stats (e.g. how many people are currently using Frost). This signal includes your Minecraft username and, if you're connected to a multiplayer server, that server's address — never your IP address, and never anything about singleplayer worlds. This is not shared publicly.

3. Third-Party Services

Frost integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Modrinth — mod distribution
  • Discord — optional community features

4. Data Retention

Diagnostic data is retained for up to 90 days. You may request deletion of any personally identifiable data by reaching out via our Discord server.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us through our Discord server.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, reach out on our Discord server.
